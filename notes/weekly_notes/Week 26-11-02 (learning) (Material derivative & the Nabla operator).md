> [!note] The Material Derivative
>  - The material derivative (also known as the Lagrangian derivative) tracks the change of a property in a single particle.
>  - This is contrasted with the Eulerian derivative which tracks the change of a property with reference to the system.
>  - They can be equated as follows:
>  $$\frac{D}{Dt} = \frac{\partial}{\partial t} + \mathbf{u} \cdot \nabla$$
