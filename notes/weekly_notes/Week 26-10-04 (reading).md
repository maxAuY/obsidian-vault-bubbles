>[!abstract] Are monodisperse phospholipid-coated microbubbles "mono-acoustic"?
> - Speikhout et al. used the "acoustical camera" method to measure the viscoelastic properties of the phospholipid shells of phospholipid-coated microbubbles.
> - They found that the microbubbles had a large range in viscosity, even with a narrow size distribution.
