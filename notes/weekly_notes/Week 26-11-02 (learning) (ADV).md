> - The nanodroplets are assumed to be uniformly distributed throughout the tissue phantom.

> [!note] Nucleation
> - Nucleation occurs when a vapour bubble inside the nanodroplet with a critical radius is created. This is the minimum radius of the gas bubble that must be created for a phase transition to become self-sustaining. 
> - This is often modelled to occur during the peak negative pressure of an ultrasound cycle. 

![[zhao_2025_dynamics_ADV_dual_frequency.pdf#page=2&rect=301,61,569,173&color=yellow|zhao_2025_dynamics_ADV_dual_frequency, p.2]]
-  The liquid is shifted to a metastable state by the negative pressure. This is when nucleation is initiated.

> [!PDF|yellow] [[qin_2021_predicting_initial_nucleation_events_during_adv_(cnt).pdf#page=2&selection=235,33,244,71&color=yellow|qin_2021_predicting_initial_nucleation_events_during_adv_(cnt), p.2]]
> > he ADV nucleation is assumed to occur by the homogeneous nucleation at the ultrasound phase where acoustic pressure values Pa(t) are the lowest and remain reasonably constant, and it is accurately described by the modified CNT

> [!PDF|yellow] [[qin_2021_predicting_initial_nucleation_events_during_adv_(cnt).pdf#page=3&selection=96,0,99,51&color=yellow|qin_2021_predicting_initial_nucleation_events_during_adv_(cnt), p.3]]
> > The occurrence of ADV nucleation in a PFP nanodroplet means that a vapor bubble nucleus is formed through spontaneous fluctuations in pure PFP liquid, which has been moved from a stable liquid state to a metastable liquid state by a decrease in pressure. 

- Nucleation has been modelled to occur when the local wave pressure drops below the vapour pressure of the perfluorocarbon minus the Laplace pressure of the nanodroplet.

![[zhou_2015_application_of_adv_in_ultrasound_therapy.pdf#page=4&rect=47,51,548,328&color=yellow|zhou_2015_application_of_adv_in_ultrasound_therapy, p.4]]

- ADV is hypothesised to be caused by the nonlinear distortion of the ultrasound wave as it travels and the focusing of the ultrasound pressure by the curvature of the nanodroplet. 

> [!PDF|yellow] [[zhou_2015_application_of_adv_in_ultrasound_therapy.pdf#page=6&selection=84,13,87,57&color=yellow|zhou_2015_application_of_adv_in_ultrasound_therapy, p.6]]
> > Acoustic droplet vaporization is initiated by a combination of two phenomena: highly nonlinear distortion of the acoustic wave before it hits the droplet and focusing of the distorted wave by the droplet itself.
> 

> [!PDF|yellow] [[zhou_2015_application_of_adv_in_ultrasound_therapy.pdf#page=6&selection=92,45,93,45&color=yellow|zhou_2015_application_of_adv_in_ultrasound_therapy, p.6]]
> > The larger the droplet size, the stronger the focusing. 

- Nucleation is predicted to occur away from the wall of the nanodroplet where the pressure is the highest. The exact location depends on the size of the droplet. 

> [!PDF|yellow] [[zhou_2015_application_of_adv_in_ultrasound_therapy.pdf#page=6&selection=95,5,99,55&color=yellow|zhou_2015_application_of_adv_in_ultrasound_therapy, p.6]]
> >  Smaller droplets (<20 μm) had the initial nucleation site in the hemisphere closer to the ultrasound source 

> [!PDF|yellow] [[zhou_2015_application_of_adv_in_ultrasound_therapy.pdf#page=6&selection=100,4,103,9&color=yellow|zhou_2015_application_of_adv_in_ultrasound_therapy, p.6]]
> > initial nucleation in larger droplets (>20 μm) formed further from the ultrasound transducer.

- Nucleation often does not occur during the first cycle.

> [!PDF|yellow] [[zhou_2015_application_of_adv_in_ultrasound_therapy.pdf#page=4&selection=164,0,179,48&color=yellow|zhou_2015_application_of_adv_in_ultrasound_therapy, p.4]]
> > Nucleation is a highly stochastic process and could occur as early as in the second pressure cycle.

> [!note] Recondensation
> - Recondensation of an activated nanodroplet can occur due to increase in pressure.
> - This can occur during the rarefactional phase or as the Laplace pressure increases when the nanodroplet grows into a microbubble.

> [!PDF|yellow] [[zhou_2015_application_of_adv_in_ultrasound_therapy.pdf#page=8&selection=324,0,338,1&color=yellow|zhou_2015_application_of_adv_in_ultrasound_therapy, p.8]]
> > A viscous fluid, limited tissue space, and high elasticity of surrounding tissue may retard the initial expansion of the gas bubble generated by ADV and re-condense the gas nucleus at lower rarefactional pressures. 
> 
> 

> [!PDF|yellow] [[zhou_2015_application_of_adv_in_ultrasound_therapy.pdf#page=7&selection=39,17,40,35&color=yellow|zhou_2015_application_of_adv_in_ultrasound_therapy, p.7]]
> > recondensation into the liquid form due to an increase in Laplace pressure.

- If a threshold radius is reached, the bubble can become stable against recondensation.
- Below this threshold, bubbles are prone to recondensation.

> [!PDF|yellow] [[zhou_2015_application_of_adv_in_ultrasound_therapy.pdf#page=7&selection=40,48,45,28&color=yellow|zhou_2015_application_of_adv_in_ultrasound_therapy, p.7]]
> > hreshold bubble radius of approximately 800 nm (in concordance with the theoretical prediction using the Homogeneous Nucleation Theory) above which the bubbles are stable against recondensation and below which uncoated bubbles are prone to recondensation.

- Recondensation can cause the efficiency of ADV to drop significantly. 
- This means that around 10x the number of nanodroplets are required to produce the same contrast as microbubble contrast agents.

> [!PDF|yellow] [[zhou_2015_application_of_adv_in_ultrasound_therapy.pdf#page=7&selection=47,6,54,24&color=yellow|zhou_2015_application_of_adv_in_ultrasound_therapy, p.7]]
> > The efficiency of initial droplet nucleation is quite high (on the order of at least 10 %), as approximately ten droplets may be sufficient for the production of a detectable amount of bubbles with comparable echogenicity as the single microbubble in sonography. However, up to 90 % of the newly formed bubbles may recondense into the liquid, resulting in the efficiency of stable bubble generation dropping to even below 1 %. 
>
